Complete Benefits Overview

Benefit Buddies packages practical health, safety, lifestyle, protection, and financial support options into a voluntary, non-insurance benefits program designed to supplement existing coverage.

Health

Virtual healthcare, mental health support, prescription savings, lab testing, imaging, fitness, vision, dental, and more.

Everyday deals, travel savings, pet care, contacts, financial wellness, tax hotline support, and family-focused services.

Virtual Healthcare

Doctors Online

  • Trusted source for medical questions and concerns.
  • 24/7 access to doctors, pharmacists, psychologists, dentists, dieticians, and more specialists.
  • Helps members replace random online searches with guidance from healthcare professionals.

NB Pet Telehealth

  • Pet parents can ask questions, discuss behavioral issues, training, and determine whether a vet visit may be needed.
  • Unlimited access to no-fee veterinary consultations for eligible household pets.
  • Around-the-clock advice, anytime and anywhere.

Online Wellness

  • Online access to health resources, personal workouts, workshops, progress trackers, and interactive wellness tools.

Telemedicine

  • Improved access to care through phone, mobile app, or video.
  • Helps reduce out-of-pocket spending by replacing certain ER and urgent care visits with on-demand care where appropriate.
  • Available for immediate family regardless of medical elections.
Vendor(s): Evo, NB Telemedicine, Recuro Health, Teladoc

Virtual Complete Care

  • Whole-person care through phone and video interactions.
  • Primary care, urgent care, and mental health options in a single platform.
  • Access to care for common issues from colds to chronic conditions.
Vendor(s): Galileo, Recuro Health

Mental Health

Counseling Services

  • Members can call 24/7 for assessment and schedule up to six free sessions by phone, virtually, or in person.
  • Support for job issues, financial or legal stress, relationships, grief, and more.
  • Immediate guidance available when urgent support is needed.

Kindly Human

  • 24/7 mental well-being support for life stressors such as relationships, finances, health, and work.
  • Members are matched with supportive peers who have similar life experiences.

LifeSpeak

  • On-demand access to educational videos and blogs for mental health and well-being challenges.
  • Resources to help managers navigate employee well-being issues.

Telephonic EAP

  • Short-term assistance for stress, depression, family problems, substance abuse, and more.
  • Referrals for childcare, eldercare, legal and financial concerns, time management, and more.
  • Organizational support for managers addressing employee issues.

Virtual Behavioral Health Counseling

  • Connects members to counselors or psychiatrists by video, mobile app, or phone.
  • Convenience, privacy, and affordability may encourage members to seek care when needed.
  • Support for common behavioral health issues, including depression and anxiety.
